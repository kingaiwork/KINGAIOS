# Third-Party Software Policy

KINGAI OS does not treat upstream software as KINGAI-owned code.

Every official image must retain or provide access to applicable third-party notices and license information. Components derived from Ubuntu, the Linux kernel, systemd, KDE/Plasma, GNU projects, firmware packages, container runtimes, AI runtimes and models remain governed by their respective terms.

Release gates must track at minimum: package/source name, version, origin, license identifier, source location, KINGAI patches, redistribution status and corresponding-source obligations where applicable.

Large third-party AI model weights are excluded from the default ISO unless redistribution rights have been explicitly verified.
