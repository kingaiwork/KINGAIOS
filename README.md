## English

### AI should feel like part of the computer, not another tab

Most AI products still live beside the operating system. Users open a browser, explain context again, move files manually and switch between disconnected applications. KINGAI OS explores a different product direction: intelligent assistance as a native part of the computing environment.

The ambition is not merely to preload an assistant. It is to create a computing experience where intelligence can progressively understand work context, help across applications, support longer tasks and connect naturally with the wider KING AI product family.

### Why now

The computer is becoming a place where increasingly capable models, local acceleration, multimodal interaction and agentic workflows can meet. At the same time, users are more sensitive to privacy, cloud dependence and uncontrolled automation. That combination creates room for an AI-native OS that treats intelligence, user authority and everyday usability as parts of one product experience.

### Product promise

**Useful computer first** — The system must remain practical even before advanced AI capabilities are used.

**AI-native experience** — Intelligence should feel integrated with work rather than bolted on as another website.

**Local-first and privacy-aware direction** — Where practical, more work can stay close to the user and device.

**Persistent work context** — Intelligent assistance becomes more valuable when projects and recurring work do not start from zero every time.

**Multimodal direction** — Text, voice, images and future interaction modes can become more natural parts of the computing relationship.

**Controlled computer-use direction** — As agent capabilities mature, selected multi-step tasks can become delegable while sensitive outcomes remain governed.

### Who it is for

- users who want a modern everyday computer with deeper intelligent assistance;
- professionals and creators managing complex digital work;
- developers and technical users exploring AI-native workflows;
- organizations evaluating more governed intelligent computing;
- server/edge users looking for a broader AI-native system family;
- device and OEM partners exploring new computing experiences.

### What success should feel like

A successful AI-native OS should reduce repeated setup, help users continue important work, make intelligent assistance feel more contextual, and remain predictable enough that the user always understands when the system is acting on their behalf.

### User journey

**Start with a familiar computing environment → bring work into one place → let intelligence assist where useful → maintain context across longer tasks → connect productivity/security/agent experiences → stay in control of sensitive outcomes.**

### Modern product directions

## 中文

### AI 不应该永远只是电脑里的另一个网页标签

今天大多数 AI 产品仍然生活在操作系统之外：打开浏览器、重复解释背景、手动搬文件、在多个应用之间切换。KINGAI OS 探索的是另一条路线：**让智能成为计算环境本身的一部分。**

目标不是简单“预装一个助手”，而是逐步建立一个能够理解工作背景、跨应用辅助、支持长期任务，并与 KING AI 其他产品自然协同的计算体验。

### 为什么是现在

本地硬件越来越强，多模态、Agent、Computer Use 等能力不断成熟，同时用户也越来越重视隐私、云依赖和自动化权限边界。这为真正 AI 原生的操作系统带来了新的产品机会。

### 产品承诺

**首先必须是一台真正好用的电脑。**  
**智能应该自然进入工作，而不是永远跳到另一个网页。**  
**本地优先和隐私意识。**  
**长期任务和项目不应该每次从零开始。**  
**多模态和 Computer Use 在成熟后逐步进入体验。**  
**敏感操作始终保持用户控制。**

### 适合谁

个人用户、专业人士、创作者、开发者、技术用户、企业、服务器/边缘用户和设备/OEM合作伙伴。

### 理想使用路径

**熟悉的计算环境 → 逐渐引入智能辅助 → 保留项目背景 → 跨应用完成更多工作 → 与 Office/Security/BOT 协同 → 对敏感操作继续保持控制。**

### 在 KING AI 中的角色

Office 是高频办公，Security 是可信保护，BOT 是受控行动，Tools 是轻量工具，OPS 是基础设施运维，而 OS 可以成为这些体验更自然的计算入口。

### 常见问题

**只是一个 AI App 吗？** 不是，方向是 AI 原生计算环境。  
**是不是所有操作都要 AI？** 不是，系统首先必须独立好用。  
**所有未来功能都已经上线吗？** 没有，未发布能力继续标注为路线图。
